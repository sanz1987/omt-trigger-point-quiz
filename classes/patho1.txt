@ Core definitions (from Nóra's study pack, not lecture slides)
The WHO defines health as:
* A state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease
- The absence of disease
- Normal vital signs
- Physical fitness only
E: Study pack, week 1 (WHO definition).

"Illness" refers to:
* The person's subjective, lived experience of disease
- The cause of a disease
- Structural tissue change
- An objective lab finding
E: Disease = disrupted structure/function with identifiable aetiology/pathogenesis. (Study pack)

Aetiology is:
* The cause(s) of a disease
- The sequence of events from cause to lesion
- Structural changes in tissues
- The patient's symptoms
E: Pathogenesis = sequence of cellular/tissue events from cause to lesion; morphology = structural changes. (Study pack)

Which is a SIGN rather than a symptom?
* Elevated measured body temperature
- Reported fatigue
- Pain described by the patient
- Nausea felt by the patient
E: Signs are objective; symptoms are subjective. (Study pack)

Which is often called the "fifth vital sign"?
* Pain
- SpO2
- Weight
- Height
E: HR, RR, BP, temperature, SpO2 and pain. (Study pack)

The biopsychosocial (ICF) frame matters to physiotherapists because:
* The same pathology does not mean the same disability
- It ignores the environment
- It only uses imaging
- It replaces clinical reasoning
E: ICF components: body functions/structures, activities, participation, environmental and personal factors. (Study pack)
